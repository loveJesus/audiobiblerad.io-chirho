## Placement and current decisions

The existing SvelteKit app remains the web player. `src/lib/catalogue-chirho/` owns source data and chapter resolution; `src/lib/player-chirho/` owns playback, position and local preferences; `src/lib/sounds-chirho/` owns the sound switch. Small synthesized WAV files live in `static/sounds-chirho/`, generated by one script. Scripture audio streams from source hosts and is never copied into this repository or build.

The catalogue writer owns its source verification and evidence. The coordinator alone writes progress rows and integrates the web/sound paths. No new dependencies or cloud resources are needed.

First-run station and passage are reversible local defaults pending owner preference: the first cleared English station, John 1. Interface text is English for this slice. No accounts, tracking or paid access are introduced. Source licence, native packaging, final app identifier, Bible Brain credentials and first publication remain open owner decisions.
